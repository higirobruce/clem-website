Email behaviour (the key requirement): on submit, the site composes and sends an email To: info@clemltd.store with BCC: clemltd1@gmail.com, subject like Proforma request — [name] — [products], body = all form fields. The visitor sees a "Thank you, we'll reply within 24 hrs" confirmation. A static site can't send email by itself — this needs either a form service (e.g. Formspree/Web3Forms) or a tiny serverless function. We'll decide the exact mechanism at build; the BCC and reply-to are configurable there.

Brand direction — suggestions

— since there's no logo or colours yet, here are starting points to react to
Colour palette options

A · Steel Blue + Amber

Industrial, trustworthy, distinctly "metal". Blue = strength/reliability, amber = energy & the CTA colour.

#1F3A5F #2E5984 #E08A1E

B · Charcoal + Red

Bold and strong — reads like heavy industry / construction. Red drives attention to the proforma CTA.

#222831 #393E46 #C0392B

C · Deep Teal + Gold

More premium / distinctive — stands apart from typical hardware suppliers while still feeling solid.

#0F3D3E #1D6F6F #D9A425

Logo concepts (wordmark + simple mark)
“CLEM” bold wordmarkStrong geometric sans (e.g. a condensed industrial face), with “LTD” small beneath. Cheapest, scales everywhere.
Mark: C + steel barA “C” formed from a folded steel sheet / I-beam cross-section. Hints at the product without being literal.
Mark: hollow sectionA square/rectangular tube outline (nod to hollow sections & door frames) framing the initials.
My recommendation to start: Option A (Steel Blue + Amber) — it photographs well behind real steel imagery and the amber gives a clear, energetic colour for every "Request Proforma" button. Pick a palette + a logo direction and I'll carry it through the high-fidelity build. We can also commission a proper logo later; a clean wordmark is enough to launch.
