About CLEM LTD

A decade of dependable steel supply in Rwanda.

Founded in 2015 and based in Kigali, CLEM LTD has grown into one of the region's trusted names in steel. We supply a complete range of steel products to contractors, fabricators, hardware retailers and individual builders across the country.

By working directly with established manufacturers in China, Tanzania, Uganda and Rwanda, we keep quality consistent and pricing competitive — and we pass that reliability on to the 2,000+ customers who count on us.

As the official representative of Steel and Tube Industries Ltd (Uganda), we bring proven, industry-grade steel to the Rwandan market.
